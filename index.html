Lesson 1

Right triangles are everywhere

Goal: understand why a diagonal can prove a rectangle is square.

The two straight sides meet at 90°. The diagonal is the hypotenuse.

a² + b² = c²
Job-site meaning: If your measured diagonal matches the calculated diagonal, your corner is square.

Try a 12' × 20' layout

What should the diagonal be?

12² + 20² = c²
144 + 400 = 544
c = √544
Hands-on assignment

Square a 12' × 20' rectangle

Set a 12' side and a 20' side. Adjust the corner until the diagonal is about 23' 3⅞". Check the opposite diagonal. When both match, the rectangle is square.
